How it works

1

Book a pickup

Pick a window that suits you. A courier collects your garments from your door — no bags to drop off, no counter to queue at.

2

A real cleaner does the work

Your order goes to a neighborhood dry cleaner, not a warehouse. They count every garment and price exactly what's in the bag.

3

Delivered back, pressed

Track both trips in the app — to the cleaner and back to you. Your clothes return ready to wear.

You approve the final price

Nobody can price a bag of laundry before opening it — so we don't pretend to. You see an estimate when you book, the cleaner counts your actual garments, and if the total comes in higher than your estimate, nothing is charged until you approve it.
